# 📊 PurchHealthcare Analysis Data Report

## 📝 Project Overview

This project analyzes healthcare cost and patient data to uncover insights related to billing,
insurance coverage, treatment affordability, and patient satisfaction. 
It provides a comprehensive overview of key financial and service metrics across diagnoses,
procedures, service types, departments, and geographic regions.

---

## 📌 Scope of Analysis

1. **Total Out-of-Pocket Costs**
2. **Total Billing Amount**
3. **Total Insurance Coverage**
4. **Total Medication Costs**
5. **Total Treatment Costs**
6. **Patient Count by Diagnosis, Procedure, Service Type, and Department**
7. **Out-of-Pocket Cost Breakdown by Diagnosis, Procedure, Service Type, and Department**
8. **Monthly Out-of-Pocket Trends**
9. **Total Patient Count**
10. **Patient Satisfaction Scores**
11. **Out-of-Pocket Analysis by City & State**

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- **Out-of-pocket expenses** remain high, especially in **January**, due to insurance deductible resets.
- **Wales** has the **highest cost burden**, while **Scotland** shows significantly **lower patient expenses**.
- Departments such as **Cardiology**, **Orthopedics**, and **General Surgery** show high patient volumes and cost shares.
- **Patient satisfaction scores are concerning**, with nearly 50% rating care poorly (scores 1 or 2).
- Clear correlation between **patient volume and monthly cost trends**, with seasonal dips and spikes.

---

## ✅ Recommendations

- Improve cost transparency and financial communication with patients.
- Analyze and replicate lower-cost strategies from regions like **Scotland**.
- Launch quality and satisfaction improvement initiatives targeting low-rated service areas.
- Promote preventive care during underutilized months like **October** to balance resources.

---

## 📣 Conclusion

By implementing these data-driven insights, we aim to enhance healthcare accessibility,
reduce patient financial burden, and improve service satisfaction. 
We look forward to further discussions on refining these strategies and executing the next phase of care optimization and system-wide improvement.
